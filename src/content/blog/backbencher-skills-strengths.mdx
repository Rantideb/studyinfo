## The Art of Spin

Admissions officers don't know you slept in class. They only know what you tell them.

## Skill 1: Last Minute Work

**Rebrand:** "Ability to work under pressure and meet tight deadlines."

Example: "During the college fest, the main sponsor pulled out 2 days before. I managed to secure a new sponsor within 24 hours." (This shows grace under fire).

## Skill 2: "Jugaad" (Street Smarts)

**Rebrand:** "Resourcefulness and Problem Solving."

Example: "When our project hardware failed, I used recycled parts to build a functioning prototype at 10% of the cost."

## Skill 3: Making Friends

**Rebrand:** "Interpersonal Skills and Team Building."

Example: "I was the bridge between the technical team and the management team, ensuring smooth communication."
