## The "Free Education" Reality

Yes, tuition is free in public universities. But getting in is tricky.

**The "APS" Certificate:** New rule for Indians. You need to verify your documents before applying. It takes months. Start NOW.

## Language Barrier?

You can study in English. But you need German to buy groceries, make friends, and get a job. Start learning A1/A2 in India. It's fun.

## Job Market

Germany needs engineers. If you are a Mechanical, Electrical, or CS engineer, you will get a job. Even if your grades were average, if you have the skills, they hire you.
