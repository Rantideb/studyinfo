## The "ROI" Argument (For Dads)

Dads love numbers. Don't talk about "dreams" and "exposure." Talk about Return on Investment (ROI).

*"Dad, if I do an MBA here in a private college, it costs 15 Lakhs. Starting salary is 4 Lakhs. It takes 4 years to recover the money. If I go to Germany, cost is 15 Lakhs (living). Starting salary is 40 Lakhs. I recover it in 6 months."*

## The "Safety" Argument (For Moms)

Moms worry about you eating and not getting kidnapped.

Show them YouTube videos of Indian students cooking dal chawal in that country. Connect them with a relative or a friend's friend who lives there. "See Mom, Sharma ji's son is there, he is fine."

## The "Settlement" Argument

Parents fear you will never come back. Be honest. Tell them you will work for a few years, earn in dollars/euros, save up, and then you have the *option* to come back rich or settle there. It's a win-win.
