## Stop "Studying" English

The reason you hate English is because you treat it like a subject. History is a subject. Math is a subject. English is a *medium*. You don't study it; you use it.

## The "Mirror Technique"

This is for shy people. Stand in front of a mirror. Talk to yourself for 2 minutes. Topic? Anything. "What did I eat today?" "Why is my boss annoying?"

No one is judging you. If you make a mistake, who cares? This builds muscle memory.

## The "Comment Section" Warrior

You spend hours on Instagram/YouTube. Use it.

Instead of scrolling, write a comment. In English. "This video was funny because..."

Engage in arguments (politely). Writing out your thoughts is the best practice.

## Think in English

This is the hardest but most important step. When you see a table, don't think "Mez" (or your native word). Think "Table."

Narrate your life in your head. "I am opening the door. I am walking to the kitchen." It sounds crazy, but it works.
