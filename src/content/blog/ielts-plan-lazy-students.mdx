## The "20-Minute Rule"

You won't study for 2 hours. Let's accept that. But you can study for 20 minutes.

**Day 1:** Do ONE Reading Passage (20 mins).

**Day 2:** Do ONE Listening Section (10 mins) + Check answers (10 mins).

**Day 3:** Write ONE Task 2 Intro and Conclusion (20 mins).

**Day 4:** Speak to yourself in the mirror on a topic (5 mins) x 4 times.

## Hack the Speaking Test

Prepare "Universal Stories."

Prepare a story about a "Time you were late." You can use this for:

- A time you missed an appointment.
- A time you were angry.
- A journey you didn't enjoy.

One story, 5 answers. Smart work, not hard work.

## The "Cambridge Books" Only

Don't buy random books. Only use the official "Cambridge IELTS" books (11 to 18). They are the real past papers. Everything else is a waste of time.
