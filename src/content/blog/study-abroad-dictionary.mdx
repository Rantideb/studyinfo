## The Basics

- **SOP (Statement of Purpose):** An essay about you. Why you want to study there.
- **LOR (Letter of Recommendation):** A letter from your teacher saying you are a good student (even if you weren't).
- **GPA (Grade Point Average):** Your marks converted to a 4.0 scale. (Usually, 60% is around 3.0, but it varies).
- **Transcript:** Official list of all your marks from college.

## The Offers

- **Conditional Offer:** "We will take you IF you get 6.5 in IELTS."
- **Unconditional Offer:** "You are in. Just pay the deposit."
- **CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies):** A document from a UK university you need for the Visa.
- **I-20:** The same thing, but for the USA.
