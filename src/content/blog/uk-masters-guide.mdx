## Why UK?

1 year Master's. You save time. You save money (living cost for 1 year vs 2 years).

**The "Russell Group" Obsession:** Everyone wants Oxford/Cambridge. But for average students, look at "Post-1992" universities. They are modern, practical, and have great industry links. (e.g., Coventry, Nottingham Trent, Oxford Brookes).

## The Process

1. **Shortlist 5 Unis:** 2 High, 2 Medium, 1 Safe.
2. **Prepare Docs:** SOP, LORs, CV, Transcripts.
3. **Apply:** Go to the university website. Click "Apply Now." Upload docs. Submit. It's free for most.
4. **Wait:** You get an offer in 2-4 weeks.
5. **Accept & Pay Deposit:** Usually £2000-£3000.
6. **CAS & Visa:** Get the CAS letter. Apply for Visa. UK Visa is points-based. If you have the CAS and money, you get the Visa.
